10-Minute Morning Routine · Quick Recap

Set your brain before the day sets it for you

Light · Cold · Breathe · Write · Move — in that order, before your phone, before coffee.

1

Sunlight in your eyes

2 min

Step outside (not through glass or sunglasses) and let natural light hit your eyes. Anchors your circadian clock, triggers a sharp morning cortisol peak, and starts the night-time melatonin timer. Cloudy day? Give it 5 minutes.

2

Cold water on face & neck

60–90 sec

Cold tap water on your face, back of the neck, and wrists. Surges norepinephrine and a slow-building dopamine while the diving reflex keeps you calm — alert but not anxious.

3

Deliberate breathwork

3 min

Inhale 4 sec → hold 2 sec → exhale 6–8 sec, with the exhale longer than the inhale. The long exhale fires the vagus nerve, lowering cortisol and quieting the amygdala for hours.

4

Written intention setting

2 min

By hand, write three things: your single most important task, one thing you look forward to, one thing you appreciate. Primes your brain's filter (RAS) and releases serotonin, oxytocin, and anticipatory dopamine.

5

Full-body movement

60 sec

One minute of anything intense — jumping jacks, squats, arm swings — to spike your heart rate. Releases BDNF ("Miracle-Gro for the brain") and wakes the thinking brain.

The one rule

No phone, no social media, no email, no news — for the full 10 minutes, ideally 30. And wait 60–90 minutes for coffee. The sequence only works if you protect it.
